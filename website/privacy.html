Parley Privacy Policy

Effective August 3, 2026

Parley is a bring-your-own-key AI chat app for iOS. It has no developer-operated backend: we run no servers, require no account, and collect no data about you. Everything described below happens either on your device or between your device and a service you choose and configure.

What we collect

Nothing. Parley contains no analytics, no tracking, no advertising SDKs, and no telemetry of any kind. We never see your conversations, your API keys, or anything else.

What stays on your device

  • Conversations are stored locally on your device using Apple's SwiftData. They are never uploaded by us.
  • API keys you enter are stored in the iOS Keychain (accessible after first unlock, not synced to iCloud). They are sent only as the authorization header on requests to the provider endpoint you configured, and are never logged.
  • Settings, including your declared birth year for the age gate, are stored in the app's local preferences.
  • Voice input is transcribed on-device by Apple's Speech framework. Audio stays in memory and is never saved or uploaded; only the text you choose to send follows the normal path of your selected provider.

Data shared with third parties at your direction

When you send a message, its content (and the conversation context needed to answer) goes to the AI provider you selected. Parley supports:

  • Apple Intelligence (on-device) — generation happens entirely on your device. Nothing is sent over the network.
  • Cloud providers — OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, DeepSeek, xAI, and OpenRouter. Your message content is sent to the provider you chose, using your own API key, under that provider's privacy policy and data-retention terms. We add no identifiers of our own.
  • Local or custom endpoints — Ollama or any OpenAI-compatible server URL you enter. Content goes only to the endpoint you configured.

When you explicitly arm web search for a message, that message's text (capped at 50 words / 400 characters, with code blocks stripped) is sent to Brave Search using your own Brave API key. Brave documents query retention of up to 90 days. Search excerpts are used to ground the answer; Parley stores only the source title, domain, and URL alongside the reply — never the raw excerpts.

Deletion

Swipe to delete any conversation in the app. Deleting the app removes all conversations, settings, and keys from your device. Because we operate no servers, there is no server-side copy to request deletion of. For data sent to a provider you configured, deletion is governed by that provider's own policy and tools.

Children

Parley is rated 17+ and requires an age declaration on first launch. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect data from anyone (of any age).
